BOOKING PROCESS
Customers book online from the service's page on the website.
1. Choose Service
2. Select Date & Time
3. Share Details
4. Get Expert
5. Work Completed

How booking works on the website:
- Open the service page, answer the few questions about the work (home type,
  area, brand, product and so on), pick an available visit date and time,
  and enter contact details and address. The booking is reviewed before it
  is confirmed.
- Plumbing, Electrician and AC Services work like a cart: add the items you
  need, then go to checkout and pick a time.
- Painting, Waterproofing and POP Ceiling show starting prices or rates for
  the choices made. Interior Design and bigger jobs are priced after a home
  visit.
- The site asks the customer to sign in or create an account at the last
  step so they can follow the booking later.
- Bigger or custom projects can ask for a free quote:
  https://supplybase.co.in/quote

After booking:
- The team confirms the booking, a professional is assigned, and the visit
  happens at the chosen time. For inspection-based work the team confirms
  the scope and final price during the visit and sends a quotation; work is
  scheduled once the customer approves it.
- Customers see every booking, its status and its details under My Bookings
  after logging in: https://supplybase.co.in/dashboard/bookings
- To change the visit time, contact the team with the booking number and the
  preferred time.

PAYMENT
- Pay online securely by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay, or pay
  the team on the day of the visit.
- If a customer starts online payment and does not finish it within 24
  hours, that booking is cancelled automatically to free the time slot.
- Any visit or consultation fee is shown in the booking flow before the
  customer confirms. The ₹99 home visit fee is adjusted in the final bill if
  the customer goes ahead with the work.
- For larger projects, payments are linked to completed stages of work,
  agreed in writing before the project starts.

CANCELLING A BOOKING
- Customers can cancel a booking themselves from My Bookings
  (https://supplybase.co.in/dashboard/bookings) until the work is scheduled.
- Once the work is scheduled, the team may already have bought material or
  booked labour, so the customer needs to call +91 91373 06446 to cancel.
- If the customer paid online and cancels, the online payment is refunded to
  the original payment method.
